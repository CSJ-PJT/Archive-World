# Archive Architecture Bible

Archive City architecture is a shared language, not a collection of copied references.

## Shared language

- **ArchiveOS / Ledger:** high-quality curtain wall, light stone podium, deep lobby, public plaza and a legible service rear.
- **Market:** fine-grain retail frontage, mixed scale, pedestrian lanes and controlled night-lighting intent.
- **Nexus:** research/production campus, utility legibility and safe service separation.
- **Logistics:** dock, truck court, container/service yard and large-parcel circulation.
- **Residential:** courtyard/piloti, community edge, school/park relationship and Korean metropolitan apartment scale.

## Building Genome rules

Each candidate combines footprint, tower, podium, facade, balcony, core, roof, entrance, material and LOD modules. It must differ from every source reference in at least seven dimensions; scale, hue, texture-only changes, source mesh copying, brand marks and real-building replication do not qualify.

## Quality chain

`Building → Family → Block → Street → District → City` is mandatory. A building can never be canonical merely because its GLB validates. Visual quality, provenance, originality, family variation, public realm and city-context reviews remain independent gates.
