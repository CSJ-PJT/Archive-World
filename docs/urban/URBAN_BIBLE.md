# Archive Urban Bible

## Urban Grammar

Urban Grammar controls block size, parcel logic, setback, coverage, floor-area proxy, spacing, pedestrian/service/vehicle networks, public realm, green network, street activation and skyline. It is evaluated by planning proxy only until an explicit city-application approval.

## District DNA

District identity is read from street and public realm before individual buildings: ArchiveOS is plaza/river-axis CBD; Market is fine-grain commerce; Nexus is a research/manufacturing campus; Logistics is freight-led; Residential is school/park/community-led; Ledger is formal financial/civic CBD; Infrastructure is corridor/terrain-led.

## City metrics

Density, walkability, green ratio, road ratio, skyline continuity, land-use mix, street activation and pedestrian continuity are planning metrics. They are not construction, transport, legal, environmental or safety certification.

## Presentation requirement

Every future review contains plan, hero, bird, human-eye, pedestrian and service views, plus a status legend that distinguishes canonical, prototype, placeholder and generated-plan-only content.
