# Building Genome Compiler Guide

The compiler translates district DNA and a deterministic seed into a placement plan. Output includes footprint, massing, facade grid and assignments, entrance and service orientation, podium, roof equipment, ground interface, materials, LOD expectations, metrics, validation rules, and provenance.

Six positive fixtures cover residential slab, point tower, mid-rise, office tower, podium, and annex. Negative fixtures prove detection of missing entrance/service/roof/side/rear/ground, excessive repetition, collision, invalid dimensions, incompatible modules, and invalid material slots.

Compilation success is a contract pass only. It does not imply visual quality, canonical approval, or city-layout authorization.
